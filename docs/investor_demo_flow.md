# Investor Demo Flow

1. Power on Pi and wait 1 minute.
2. Ensure you are on the same WiFi network.
3. Navigate to `http://raspberrypi.local:8000`
4. Explain the "Multispectral CBC Imaging" architecture.
5. Emphasize that this is a research prototype.
6. Click "Run Multispectral Demo Scan".
7. Show how the 5 simulated wavelengths are "captured" and appear in the gallery.
8. Explain the future AI pipeline.
